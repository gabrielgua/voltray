Bug fix release.

## Fixed

- **Taskbar mode no longer shows over full-screen apps.** When Voltray started at sign-in before the taskbar was ready, it fell back to a window of its own that stayed on top of everything, games and full-screen videos included. It now waits for the taskbar and attaches to it, never floating on its own.
- **Hidden whenever the taskbar is.** The devices on the taskbar disappear when the taskbar auto-hides or when an app is full screen on that monitor, and come back with it.

## Download

- **Voltray-0.1.2-setup.exe**: installer (updates an earlier version in place; your settings are kept).
- **Voltray-0.1.2-portable.zip**: no installation; extract and run `Voltray.exe`.

The files are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
